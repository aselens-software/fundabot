### 🌐 Advanced Web Dashboard
> *Say goodbye to complex setup commands! Funda comes with a sleek, user-friendly Web Dashboard.*

* **Real-Time Configuration:** Enable or disable modules, set up channels, and manage permissions with just a few clicks.
* **Server Stats & Logs:** Keep track of your server's health and activity through an intuitive visual interface.
* **Custom Prefix & Language:** Fully customizable to fit your community's identity.

---

### 🎵 Premium Music Experience
> *Experience lag-free, high-fidelity music streaming in your voice channels.*

* **High-Quality Audio:** Supports multiple platforms with crystal clear sound.
* **Custom Audio Filters:** Enhance your listening experience with filters like Bassboost, Nightcore, 8D, and more.
* **Playlists & 24/7 Support:** Save your favorite tracks and keep the vibe going around the clock.

> *Keep your community active and engaged with an addictive RPG-like economy.*

* **Custom Profile Cards:** Users can level up and showcase their progress with beautifully generated rank cards.
